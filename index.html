Manifesto

Not another forecast.
Your surf memory.

A forecast tells you what the sea will do. swell.blue keeps what it did, and what it did to you.

Objective readings of the water sit next to your own words about the session. Over time the two start to explain each other: which swell period you love, which wind ruins a Tuesday, which board you keep reaching for.
